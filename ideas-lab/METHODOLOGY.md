## Key insight
Visible gaps (trade press, public archives, academic domains) are productized within months. Only three knowledge pools remain reliably unexploited: (a) tedium-dismissed ideas, (b) gated practitioner knowledge, (c) team-specific tacit knowledge. All future generation flows from these.
