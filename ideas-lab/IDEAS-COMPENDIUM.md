# PART 1 — CONFIRMED IDEAS (10/10, high confidence)

## 1. WikiPulse — Wikipedia revision-velocity emerging-tech signal feed
Cheap subscription API detecting emerging technologies weeks early from Wikipedia revision-history velocity, article-creation and contributor-influx signals (>20TB dumps). Positioned as a supplementary raw-signal feed, not a platform. **Buyer:** corporate-innovation scouts, solo-GP VCs, CVC analysts. **Why it passes:** no product uses Wikipedia edit-velocity (verified twice; GitDealFlow checked = GitHub-only); category demand proven (CB Insights ~$47K/yr median, Quid, Gartner); narrow single-signal feeds demonstrably monetize (Evertrace, GitDealFlow).

## 2. PlotFolio — portfolio analytics for death-care PE roll-ups
Cross-portfolio analytics for PE consolidators of cemeteries/funeral homes: inventory depletion forecasting, pre-need optimization, land-use planning, acquisition benchmarks. **Buyer:** PE roll-ups (SCI $181M/2024 acquisitions; deathcare M&A at two-decade high). **Why it passes:** PlotBox/CemSites are single-site operational tools — no portfolio-level vendor exists; data TB-scale (millions of plot records, scanned deeds, GIS/LiDAR surveys). Ethically reframed from dynamic-pricing original.

## 3. LienLift — self-storage lien-auction recovery optimization
Per-unit auction price prediction (CV on unit photos + hedonic models) with reserve/timing/channel recommendations, lifting the documented 39¢-on-the-dollar recovery rate. **Buyer:** operators/REITs (18,500+ facilities, 40k+ paid auctions/month via StorageTreasures). **Why it passes:** industry press explicitly frames AI price prediction as an unsolved opportunity; Ai Lean ($5M raised) is adjacent workflow automation only.

## 4. AquiferIQ — data-driven well-siting for municipal & irrigation water
ML yield/depth prediction from state digitized water-well drilling-log databases + USGS aquifer/terrain layers, replacing/de-risking $50–100k test wells and five-figure hydrogeology consulting. V1 scoped to digital-log states (CA WCR, TX SDR). **Buyer:** municipal water authorities, irrigation districts, drillers. **Why it passes:** no commercial ML well-siting product in the US (MapAid = nonprofit East-Africa tool; Leapfrog/GMS = expert modeling tools; Wellntel = sensors); direct spend evidence ($96,850 municipal siting contract in board records).

## 5. RowGuard — automated easement-conflict detection for utility/pipeline ROW
NLP parses county deed/easement records into geometry, overlays on utility GIS to pre-flag encroachments and conflicting easements before construction. **Buyer:** utilities, pipeline operators, renewables developers (ROW = 5–10% of pipeline project cost). **Why it passes:** Pandell/Quorum/geoAMPS are records/workflow systems — none detect conflicts from deed text; deed-text→geometry is commercially proven (DeedPlotter AI etc.); TB-scale scanned deed archives at build phase.

## 6. ParcelPast — per-parcel historical-imagery change-detection reports
AI change detection over public-domain georeferenced historical imagery (USGS DOQ 1987-2006 + NAIP 2003-present ≈ 38 years national coverage) producing per-parcel reports for title insurers, attorneys and Phase I ESA consultants. **Buyer:** ESA consultants (historical aerial review MANDATORY under ASTM E1527-21), title/legal. **Why it passes:** verified twice — NETR = manual viewer; LightBox/EDR = PDFs for manual review; CAPE Analytics = recent-imagery insurance product. NETR's 1,000TB archive is a licensing upside, not a dependency.

## 7. MitigationWatch — imagery monitoring & reporting for wetland mitigation banks
Hybrid remote-sensing service for banks' mandatory 5–10-year USACE monitoring: drone/satellite time-series tracks vegetation/hydrology/encroachment between required field visits and auto-assembles district-compliant annual reports. **Buyer:** mitigation bankers, in-lieu-fee programs, DOTs. **Why it passes:** Ecobot = field-forms SaaS (explicitly field-centric); RES flies drones in-house (practice, not product); ERDC drone-truthing = research; monitoring spend is compulsory; multi-TB imagery portfolio.

## 8. RailTitle — the digital rail-corridor title layer
Digitize, georeference and AI-index the ICC railroad valuation archive (RG 134: ~125,000 ROW/tract maps, largely undigitized at NARA) into the national rail-corridor title layer; per-case packages for Trails Act takings attorneys, corridor lessees (fiber), utilities, DOTs, title insurers. **Why it passes:** classic schlep — no product or national-intent project exists; TRG/Westmoreland are manual per-case retrieval services (= demand proof); MnDOT/ELHS partial projects prove georeferencing feasibility; ~11,000 cubic feet → multi-TB.

## 9. CornerVault — national PLSS corner-record & monument tie-sheet archive
Aggregates and AI-indexes the millions of survey corner records fragmented across county offices in ~30 PLSS states; subscription evidence-search for 40k+ land surveyors. **Why it passes:** no national aggregator; cp-db.com (single-state Colorado subscription product) proves surveyors pay for exactly this model; Indiana's official attempt stalled at 5/92 counties; single-digit-TB document archive; county-sourcing schlep is the moat.

## 10. StrikeMap — NEXRAD analytics for FAA Wildlife Hazard Assessments
Fuses per-airport NEXRAD bird-activity climatology (published BirdCast-class methods), FAA strike-database analytics and attractant mapping into the quantitative sections of mandated Part 139 WHAs/WHMP reviews. **Buyer:** WHA producers (USDA APHIS — 872 airports on cost-recovery; consultants) + 500+ certificated airports; $65,140 documented single-contract price point. **Why it passes:** regulatory standard is field-survey + strike-DB only; AHAS = real-time tactical avoidance (different deliverable); ProDiGIQ = strike logging; Robin/DeTect = hardware; NEXRAD archive ~250-270TB compressed.
