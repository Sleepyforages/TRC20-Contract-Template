# ObituaryMind — final verdict: KILLED (was parked)

- Novelty compromised at mechanism level: Legacy.com MarketIQ (acquired Obitdata.com 2017; operating since 2014) sells obituary-volume market-share analytics to funeral homes — obituary-volume B2B data products exist.
- Remaining whitespace (predictive 30-day supply-side forecasting for casket/vault manufacturers) has demand evidence of exactly one earnings-call anecdote (Matthews International attributing a casket-volume miss to early flu season) + one unvalidated vendor pitch (Explorai). SCI discloses death-rate variability as an accepted risk, not a problem they pay to solve.
- Verdict: cannot reach high confidence on novelty or demand → KILL.
- Learning: "parked" ideas need a mechanism-level novelty check (obituary-volume analytics existed even though *forecasting* didn't) — same-mechanism-different-deliverable counts against novelty.
